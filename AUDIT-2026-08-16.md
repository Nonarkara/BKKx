## E. Changes from the prior audit worth your eye

1. **`/shophouses/manuscript` is now 404.** The prior audit (2026-08-15) said
   "All routes 200 except /shophouses/manuscript at the wrong prefix." Now the
   route itself is 404. Per commit `31b9682`: "the manuscript is removed from
   the site accordingly, and the essay page opens by saying plainly that it
   is the companion, not the chapter." The manuscript is now a separate DOCX
   deliverable. If you want the route to return something (a "this essay
   became a chapter, here's the DOCX" stub), it's a 10-line page add.

2. **No `/shophouses/atlas` route.** The pressure map is embedded in the
   essay at the `sh-atlas` section. There is no deep-link URL to the map
   itself. If you want one (so a researcher can paste
   `shophouses.nonarkara.org/shophouses/atlas` into an email), it's a
   one-page route that renders only the `PressureMap` and the quadrant
   legend.

3. **The wrangler deployments list shows `bkkx-site` at v4e7b45bc
   (2026-08-04)** while the live HTML is the new build. The list is either
   stale, capped, or tracking a different deployment path. The fact that
   `bkkx-site.drnon.workers.dev/shophouses` returns the new HTML is the
   truth on the wire.

4. **12 photos in `site/public/shophouses/photos/`, all consumed by the essay.**
   None used on the Bible, none on the manuscript (the manuscript route is
   gone). If you ever want to publish a 12-page "shophouse photo essay" you
   have the assets ready and not in use anywhere.

5. **The 4-quadrant framing is now the load-bearing argument of the essay.**
   The numbers are real (computed, not asserted), they sum correctly
   (268+888+884+271=2,311), and they are reproducible (`build-shophouse-pressure.py`
   is 191 lines, runs in <1s, no external API calls). Every figure on the
   atlas section traces to the script.

6. **Phra Nakhon + Samphanthawong = 85% of the 268 "worth reconsidering"
   buildings.** This is the essay's spatial claim and it is exact, not
   hand-waved. If you want to make the district break-down the front of
   the atlas, it is one bar-chart away.

7. **The 29 vs 2,311 gap is the strongest single line in the essay.** It
   is a number a reader can hold in their head. Recommend keeping it as a
   hero stat in the atlas section.

8. **The Bible's "spec sheet" feel is genuinely fixed.** Quick-reference
   card (8 numbers up front), dimensional signature (5.0 × 13.5 m median
   shown as actual histogram, not asserted), gazetteer (29 entries grouped
   by evidence tier, not alphabetised). The page now reads as a research
   instrument rather than a reference manual.
