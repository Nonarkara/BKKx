## C. atlas branch — what I can verify

The "atlas branch" exists as **two separate systems** that share the word "atlas" but
no code or deploy pipeline. The summary at session start conflated them. I separate
them here because the user has effectively three surfaces, not one.

### C.1 The standalone atlas at `atlas.nonarkara.org`

This is `Nonarkara/bkk-3d-atlas` — a **separate repo, not checked out on this machine**.
CLAUDE.md in `BKKxCulture/` confirms: "The operational digital twin at atlas.nonarkara.org
is a fully separate system: `Nonarkara/bkk-3d-atlas`, its own repo, its own Worker."

What I can verify from the live wire:

- **Live**: `atlas.nonarkara.org/` returns 200, 31 KB vanilla HTML, title "BKKx 3D Atlas —
  Bangkok block by block", 432,077 OSM buildings, MapLibre GL 4.7.1 from unpkg,
  ArcGIS Online imagery, OpenFreeMap tiles, HLS.js for camera streams. Stack: vanilla
  HTML + JS, **not** Next.js.
- **Query handling**: `?at=100.4935,13.7520,14.2` returns 200 but the wire HTML is the
  same home page — any fly-to behavior is in the JS bundle, which I cannot audit
  without the source.
- **POI service**: `atlas.nonarkara.org/pois/oldtown.geojson` → **404**. The
  standalone atlas does not serve the bkk POI GeoJSONs. Neither does
  `/poi/oldtown` nor `/data/oldtown.geojson`. Same 404 for any of the 5 data.go.th
  layers (temples, royal-temples, national-museums, national-libraries, national-archives)
  — they are served only by `bkkx-site`, not the standalone atlas.

**What I cannot audit** (source not in this checkout):

- The standalone atlas's POI registry, layer toggles, marker clustering
- Whether its 3D building layer is in sync with the 2,311 Overture footprint candidates
  on bkkx-site
- Its ?at= fly-to logic
- Its district boundary polygons

**Recommendation**: clone `Nonarkara/bkk-3d-atlas` to do a proper code-level audit. Until
then, treat the standalone atlas as a black box — verified live, unverified source.

### C.3 Divergence between the two

The two systems do **not** share POI data, layer toggles, or 3D state. The bkkx-site
atlas (in Next.js) is the one with the 6 POI layers; the standalone atlas has its own
data pipeline. The summary's claim "bkk iframe shows dark Console atlas" is true of
the bkkx-site AtlasView, not of the standalone at atlas.nonarkara.org. A user landing on
atlas.nonarkara.org directly sees a different product than a user who loads
bkk.nonarkara.org and reads the front door.
